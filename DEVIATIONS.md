# Deviations from PREREGISTRATION.md

Every change to the pre-registered plan made after the pre-registration commit is logged here: date (BST) · what changed · why · who approved. The plan file itself is never silently edited.

| Date | Change | Reason | Approved by |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-27 | **DeepLift is removed from the main study.** The confirmatory set is now Random, Attention, InputXGrad, IG and LIME. DeepLift stays available with `--with_deeplift` for an appendix note. | CPU smoke test on real models (val split, 6 examples, `results` not committed). The comprehensiveness scores of Captum `DeepLift` correlate with those of `InputXGradient` at r = 0.98 on XLM-R (81% of cells identical) and r = 0.94 on BanglaBERT. Captum's DeepLift rules are therefore not being applied to the Hugging Face modules (custom GELU, functional softmax), so the method collapses to a gradient × (input − baseline) variant. Reporting it as DeepLift would misrepresent the method. Decided before any test-split data was seen; H1/H2 are unchanged apart from the smaller method set. | Team, via R. H. Tamim, 2026-11-27 |
| 2026-11-27 | **Pre-data amendment: PREREGISTRATION v0.2 → v1.0.** The primary study is now a ground-truth shortcut protocol (Bastings et al., 2022) with controlled subword fragmentation: a cross-tokenizer control (same words: 1 piece in BanglaBERT, 4 pieces in XLM-R) plus within-model contrasts. New metrics: Precision@k and coverage rank, plus agreement with AOPC. Added `word_mean`, insertion at random interior positions, and a matched reliance gate (flip rate ≥ 0.90, difference ≤ 0.05). The v0.2 real-data analysis becomes secondary (3 seeds). | Critical review found that v0.2 could show only that protocols *disagree*, not which one is *correct*; a 2026 preprint (ICE, arXiv:2603.18579) already shows operator choice flips conclusions. Ground truth makes the claims verifiable. **No test-split data had been inspected or scored.** Only validation-split smoke tests had been run. v0.2 stays in git history (commit 4da125e). | Team (via R. H. Tamim), 2026-11-27; supervisor sign-off pending |
| 2026-11-27 | **v1.0 → v1.1: chance-corrected ground-truth metrics.** cP@k = (P@k − k/N)/(1 − k/N) and cCover = (E_rand − cover/N)/(E_rand − k/N), with E_rand = k(N+1)/(N(k+1)). Undefined (example excluded and counted) when N ≤ k. These are now the primary scores; raw scores become secondary. | Under random ranking, raw P@k and coverage rank depend on k, the number of ground-truth pieces, so 1-piece and 4-piece conditions are not comparable. The validation smoke run made this visible in the **Random baseline only** (raw subword P@k 0.28 at 4 pieces vs 0.11 at 1 piece). The correction follows from probability theory and was checked by Monte-Carlo simulation (mean ≈ 0). **H1–H3 wording, directions, contrasts and decision rules are unchanged** (no HARKing), even though validation numbers for one contrast run against H1. No test-split data seen. | Team (via R. H. Tamim), 2026-11-27 |
| 2026-11-27 | **Shortcut words frozen as selected** (`data/shortcut/shortcuts.json`; no overrides). | Team review: all 6 judged to be real, label-neutral Bangla words. | Team, 2026-11-27 |
